The Math Behind the Cycle
Bitcoin's halving cuts miner rewards every ~1,460 days. After each halving it takes ~1,070 days for the demand surge to push to a new ATH, then ~364 days of bear market (distribution → denial → capitulation → despair → bottom). The cycle score (0–100) measures where we are. Under 40 is historically the best time to buy.
