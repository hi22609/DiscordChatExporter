The Whole Thing, In Plain English
📒
What is a "blockchain"?
▶

Imagine a notebook that everyone in the world shares a copy of. Every time someone sends money, a new line is written in every copy at the same time.

Because thousands of people hold identical copies, no single person can secretly erase a line or fake one — the other copies would instantly disagree and reject the lie.

🟢 TL;DR: A blockchain is a shared notebook that nobody can secretly edit.
📦
Why "block" + "chain"?
▶

Transactions don't get written one at a time. They're bundled into a block — like a page in the notebook — roughly every 10 minutes.

Each new page carries a fingerprint of the page before it. Change one old page and its fingerprint changes, which breaks every page after it. That's the chain — pages locked together in order, all the way back to 2009.

🟢 TL;DR: Blocks are pages; the chain glues them so history can't be rewritten.
⛏️
What is "mining"?
▶

To add the next page, computers around the world race to solve a hard math puzzle. The winner gets to write the page — and earns brand-new bitcoin as a reward.

The puzzle is hard to solve but instant to check. That's the trick: it costs real electricity to cheat, but it's free for everyone else to verify the winner played fair.

🟢 TL;DR: Miners burn electricity to earn the right to add the next block — and get paid in BTC.
🔑
What is a wallet & a "key"?
▶

Your bitcoin isn't a file on your phone — it's just an entry in that shared notebook. Your wallet holds a secret private key: a password only you know that proves the entry is yours.

Sign with your key and the network accepts your transaction. Lose the key and the coins are frozen forever. This is why people say "not your keys, not your coins."

🟢 TL;DR: Your key is the master password to your money — guard it like your life.
🛡️
Why can't it be hacked or faked?
▶

To rewrite history, an attacker would need to out-compute every honest miner on Earth — at the same time — and keep doing it forever. The electricity bill alone would be billions of dollars per day.

It's not "unhackable" by magic. It's protected by making cheating cost more than it could ever pay. Honesty is simply the profitable move.

🟢 TL;DR: Cheating costs more than it's worth, so the network stays honest by design.
💎
Why is it valuable?
▶

There will only ever be 21 million bitcoin — written into the code, impossible to change. No government can print more, no bank can freeze it, and it moves anywhere on Earth in minutes.

It's the first money in history that is provably scarce and fully digital. That combination — like gold you can email — is what the whole cycle is built on.

🟢 TL;DR: Fixed supply + global + unstoppable = digital gold.
