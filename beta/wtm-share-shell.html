Questions.

Is this the real app?
It's the real interface running a simulated Friday night. Joining, chatting, stories and the map all work. The production app connects to real people and real events.
Does it track me?
No. No account, no location permission, no analytics. Nothing you do here leaves your device unless you choose to send feedback.
Which cities?
Pittsburgh first. The demo map covers the whole metro, from Cranberry down to McKeesport.
How do I get in?
Beta access spreads through friends. If someone sent you this, you're already on the path. Leave feedback with your name and you're on the list.
