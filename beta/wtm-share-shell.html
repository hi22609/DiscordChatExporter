Questions

Quick answers.

Is this the real app?
This is a fully interactive demo of the WTM beta: real interface, real map, simulated city activity. Everything you tap works: joining moves, chats, stories, the map. The production app connects to real people and real events.
Is my data safe in this demo?
Nothing you do in the demo leaves your device. No account, no location access, no tracking. Feedback is only shared if you choose to send it.
What cities does WTM cover?
The beta is Pittsburgh-first. The demo map covers the whole metro, from Cranberry to McKeesport. More cities are on the roadmap.
How do I get in?
Beta access rolls out through friends. If someone sent you this demo, you're already on the path. Leave feedback with your name and you're on the list.
