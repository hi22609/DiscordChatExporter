# WTM Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE] · **Contact:** privacy@whatsthemove.app

WTM ("What's The Move") is a social map for finding and posting local events
("Moves") and places ("Spots"). This policy explains what we collect, why,
and your choices. Short version: we collect what the app needs to work,
we don't sell your data, and you can delete your account any time.

## What we collect

- **Account info** — email, username, display name, and optional profile
  photo and bio. Required to create an account.
- **Location** — your device location (with your permission) while you use
  the app, to show moves and spots near you. We do **not** track location in
  the background, and we do not store a history of your movements. Move and
  spot locations you post are stored because that's the product.
- **Content you post** — moves, spots, photos, RSVPs, fire votes, saves.
- **Invite graph** — which member's invite code you used to join. This is
  core to how WTM works.
- **Device info** — a push notification token (if you enable notifications)
  and basic diagnostics (app version, crash logs).

## What we do NOT collect

Contacts, background location, precise location history, advertising
identifiers. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

## How we use it

To operate the service: show nearby content, deliver notifications (e.g.,
someone joined your move), enforce our rules (rate limits, blocks, reports),
and fix crashes.

## Who we share it with

Service providers that run WTM's infrastructure, bound by their own
agreements: **Supabase** (database, auth, storage), **Expo** (push
notifications, app services), **Google Maps** (map tiles and geocoding).
We disclose data if legally required. If WTM is acquired, data transfers
with the service under this policy.

## What others see

Your username, display name, photo, bio, city, moves and spots you post,
and your attendance on public moves are visible to other members. Your
email is never public. Blocking a member hides your content from each other
both ways.

## Retention & deletion

Content lives until you delete it or your account. To delete your account
and all associated data, email **privacy@whatsthemove.app** from your
account email or use the in-app option; deletion completes within 30 days.

## Age

WTM is for users **17 and older**. We do not knowingly collect data from
anyone under 17; if we learn we have, we delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) you may have rights to
access, correct, export, or delete your data. Email
**privacy@whatsthemove.app** and we'll honor them.

## Changes

We'll post changes here and update the effective date. Material changes get
an in-app notice.
